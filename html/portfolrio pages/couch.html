Sleeping Riso Print, 2025

This piece was the first drawing I made explicitly for the risograph process. I used this as a way to familiarize myself with the process and tried to overall just have fun with it. The piece serves as a self portrait in an abstract way. depicting myself as a very cartoony character and more so showing off parts of what I view as important parts of myself, such as the posters on the wall being media that personally impacted me and plushies.
